## * Q&A

For inquiries, please access https://ciss.konkuk.ac.kr/ciss/18314/subview.do or go to ciss.konkuk.ac.kr → Student Support 유학생 지원 → Q&A.

Please log in using your portal ID and password to submit your question.

### Email consultations are not available.

## * Potal ID Log-In

Many application procedures related to campus life, including dormitory applications and submission of TOPIK score reports (required for graduation), are conducted through the notices on the Information Board. Students are required to log in with their portal ID to submit applications.

Detailed instructions are as follows.

A student's portal email address is created by adding @konkuk.ac.kr to the student's university portal ID.

(Example: If a student's portal ID is abcd, the Konkuk University email account is abcd@konkuk.ac.kr.  Students may also access their university email account via kumail.konkuk.ac.kr.)

For testing purposes, if you click https://forms.office.com/r/2RmHNmjqTF (Test page),  two login screens will appear, as shown on the right.

After successfully logging in on both login screens, you will be able to use the application websites operated by the Konkuk University International Student Center.
